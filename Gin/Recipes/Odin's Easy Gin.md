#### Ingredients
- 1 liter of 43% ABV neutral
- Skin of 1 tangerine
- 12 grams of juniper berries
- 3 grams of coriander seeds
#### Process
1. Mildly crush the juniper berries and the coriander seeds
2. Add the tangerine skin, juniper berries and coriander seeds to the neutral
3. Let it macerate at room temperature for two weeks then filter out herbs, skins and berries **OR** let it macerate max 24 hours and don't filter it
4. Distill the macerated gin slowly in a pot still
5. Discard the first 10 ml
6. Collect the next 400 ml, which will be around 70-80% ABV
7. Dilute to 45% ABV
8. Let it rest for 5 weeks in glass.
