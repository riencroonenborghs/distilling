#### Ingredients
- 500 ml water
- 2 tbsp juniper berries, lightly crushed
- 1 tsp coriander seeds
- ½ tsp black peppercorns (or grains of paradise)
- 2 green cardamom pods, cracked
- 1 small strip of cinnamon or cassia bark (about 1 cm)
- Peel of 1 lemon and ½ orange, no white pith
- 1 sprig rosemary (optional)
- 1 tsp vegetable glycerin (optional, for body)
#### Method
1. Toast the dry spices (juniper, coriander, pepper, cardamom, cinnamon) in a dry pan for 1-2 minutes until fragrant, then crush lightly.
2. Add the water and simmer gently, covered, for 10 minutes.
3. Add the citrus peel and rosemary for the last 2 minutes.
4. Take off the heat, cover, and steep for 1-2 hours, or overnight in the fridge for a stronger flavor.
5. Strain through a coffee filter or fine cloth, then stir in the glycerin.
6. Store in the fridge for up to 1-2 weeks.
#### Serve
Mix 50-60 ml of concentrate with 120-150 ml tonic over ice, and add a twist of lemon or grapefruit peel. Taste and adjust, since the concentrate’s strength varies with how long you steep.
### Variations

- **Garden:** Add cucumber ribbons, fresh mint, and a few basil leaves during the last 5 minutes of steeping.
- **Floral:** Add 1 tsp dried lavender or elderflower and a few dried hibiscus petals after you take it off the heat. Steep only 30 minutes or it turns bitter.
- **Grapefruit & rosemary:** Double the rosemary, swap the orange for grapefruit peel, and add a squeeze of fresh grapefruit juice when serving.
- **Spicy:** Add a few extra peppercorns and a slice of fresh ginger.
